Why your Facebook ads aren’t bringing enquiries

Boosting posts without a clear plan can leave you spending more and learning little.

4 min read · By Sumit Karmacharya
META ADS

Before spending more, look at the campaign as a customer would. Are the ads reaching relevant people? Is the offer clear? Does the message explain why someone should enquire?

Check the path from ad to enquiry

Your ad should explain your offer and give people a clear next step. Make sure the page or form they reach continues the same conversation and is easy to complete.

Learn from the campaign

Review audience response and enquiry quality, not just activity. Use performance tracking to guide campaign improvements.

AI tools can support research and creative testing, while your business goals guide the decisions.

Make each campaign a useful way to learn what connects your business with potential customers.
