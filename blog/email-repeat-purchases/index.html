How email can encourage repeat purchases

Past customers may need a useful reason to return. Keep the conversation going with thoughtful follow-ups.

4 min read · By Sumit Karmacharya
EMAIL MARKETING

Email can help you stay connected with customers after an enquiry or purchase. Share useful information and introduce relevant offers when they make sense.

Make follow-up useful

Welcome new subscribers, follow up with interested customers, and keep past customers informed about things that may matter to them.

Choose messages around customer needs

Promotional and repeat-purchase campaigns work best when they are relevant. Shape your messages around what customers value and give them a clear next step.

Keep improving

Track results and make improvements over time. AI can support content creation and testing, with messages still shaped around your customers’ needs.
